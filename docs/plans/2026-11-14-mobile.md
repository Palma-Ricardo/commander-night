# Commander Night — Mobile & small screens

**Status:** engineering done 2026-11-14. The exit criterion is a full game on a phone (see §6).
**Touches:** `table/` (input + compact layout), `client/` (compact layout + long-press), the
`@night/core` harness (`SceneActor.screen`), `e2e/touch.spec.ts`. No protocol or bridge change.

## 1. The problem

Both clients rendered on a phone, but neither was playable there.

- **Table client: you could not cast anything.** Casting is a drag (prompts-on-the-table §9), and a
  plain tap on a playable hand card is deliberately ignored (Issues.txt #10: no stray casts).
  The drag also never armed on touch. `maybeBeginDrag` bails without a tracked pointer, and the
  pointer was only tracked from canvas `pointermove`. A finger delivers no move before `pointerdown`.
- **Hover-only reading.** The table client reads cards through the Alt-gated preview panel and the hand
  hover-lift. Neither exists on touch.
- **Wheel-only hand scroll.** It also scrolls with ←/→, which a phone doesn't have either.
- **Mana-only abilities were unreachable in the table client.** There was no canvas context menu at
  all. In the classic client they needed a right-click.
- **Other mouse- and keyboard-only paths:** Esc was the only camera-pin release, and DistributePanel −1
  was right-click.
- **Layout:**
  - On a 390 px screen the table's log rail (≥ 288 px) plus the compass reserve (180 px) left
    the felt a camera scale of about 0.0006.
  - The classic grid gave the board about 50 px.

## 2. Decisions

| | Decision | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Touch cast | **Tap → inspect sheet → Cast.** Drag-to-cast also fixed for touch | Still two deliberate steps, so #10's guarantee holds; a finger drag on a 98 px hand band is fiddly |
| Orientation | **Table: landscape-first**, portrait works with a dismissible "rotate" hint. Classic: both | Four quadrants are a landscape object; the classic DOM reflows |
| Branching | Per-gesture **`pointerType`**, not device detection | A touchscreen laptop gets mouse behavior from its mouse and touch behavior from its finger |
| Compact | `(max-width: 900px), (max-height: 500px)` — CSS `COMPACT_QUERY` and the pure `isCompactViewport` agree | The canvas fills the window, so the felt math and the DOM chrome flip together, and the layout stays unit-testable |

## 3. Table client: gestures

`scene/gesture.ts` is Pixi-free and unit-tested. It classifies a press on a card:

| Press | Verdict | Effect |
|---|---|---|
| Released inside the slop | tap | Mouse: unchanged. Touch: a playable hand/command card, or any card with no click meaning, opens the **inspect sheet**; otherwise the normal click |
| Touch held ≥ 450 ms inside the slop | longPress | Inspect sheet on any face-up card; the trailing tap is swallowed |
| Touch leaves the slop mostly sideways on the hand fan / carousel strip | hScroll | The row follows the finger (same clamp as the wheel) |
| Anything else leaving the slop | drag | Cast lift / point drag, as before |
| Mouse right-click | — | Inspect sheet (desktop's mana-ability menu, which it never had) |

`handleActorDown` records the pointer from the press itself. This is the one-line root cause of "can't
cast". Touch never fires the hover lift or the Alt preview, because touch fires `pointerover` on contact.
A lifted finger clears the tracked pointer, so the per-frame hover authority can never hold a preview.

**The inspect sheet** (`chrome/InspectSheet.tsx`) shows one card, large, with buttons derived purely from
the shared affordance by `chrome/inspect-actions.ts`:
- **Cast / Play / Activate / Use…** for a play action;
- **Tap for mana** for manaOnly;
- **Choose as / Unselect target**, **Pay with this**, **Attack / Block / Remove …**.

Every button answers through `interact.objectClick` (or `answerObject` for mana), so it adds no new
meaning. The sheet ignores all clicks until it has seen a press of its own. It opens under a finger
that is still down, and that finger's lift otherwise clicked the backdrop, or the Close button that a
hand card's sheet puts right under the thumb. Live-caught by the touch spec.

Also:
- The camstate badge is a **Follow** button.
- Log card references open the sheet on click.
- DistributePanel chips get a visible −.
- Menus close on outside `pointerdown`.

## 4. Table client: compact layout

- **Log:** starts folded on a phone. Open, it is an overlay drawer that reserves nothing (no
  `setLogInset`, `--rail-w` stays the tab width), so the felt never re-frames to peek at the log.
- **Compass:** folds to one small button showing the viewed seat. The camera reserves
  `HUD_RIGHT_OVERHANG_COMPACT` (56) instead of 180 for a right seat, and a 6 px safe margin.
- **Hand band:** the floor is `min(150, 0.25·h)`, which is continuous at h = 600, so desktop is
  unchanged. That gives 98 px at 390 tall instead of 150 (40% of the screen).
- **Stack front card:** `min(0.33·h, 0.25·w)`. The width bound only bites when w < 1.32·h (portrait); a
  third of 844 was a 200 px card whose gutter left the felt about 70 px.
- **CSS:**
  - a compact `@media` block covers 44 px targets, the corner rows, right-anchored scrolling
    menus, centre panels capped to the screen, `100dvh`, and `env(safe-area-inset-*)`
    (`viewport-fit=cover`);
  - the in-game status bar hides while the connection is healthy.
- **Fullscreen:** a Table-menu item where the Fullscreen API exists, plus `manifest.webmanifest`
  (`display: fullscreen`, landscape) so Add to Home Screen gets past the URL bar on iPhone.
  Auto-mana folds into that menu (☰) on a phone.
- **Render resolution:** capped at 2× (phones report 3×).

The camera and layout invariants now also run at four landscape phone sizes
(`SUPPORTED_VIEWPORTS`) and two portrait ones (sane scale).

## 5. Classic client

- **Layout:**
  - one column; the log/chat is a drawer behind a header button with the unread badge;
  - opponents are a horizontal scroll-snap row;
  - `--card-unit` shrinks, the hand scrolls sideways, and hover zoom is limited to `(hover: hover)`;
  - same pinned contract as C5: the dock and hand are never below the fold.
- **Long-press** (`ui/cards/long-press.ts`) on a card shows the preview and opens the card menu, so
  mana-only abilities are reachable. It swallows the lift's click (which would otherwise cast). The
  preview now follows a real mouse only. A phone fakes `pointerenter` on every tap, which
  popped a preview each time you played a card.

## 6. Verification

- **Unit:** table +20 (gesture, inspect-actions, phone framing, band floor).
- **`e2e/touch.spec.ts`:** an emulated phone (`hasTouch`, `isMobile`) using real touch events only.
  - Table (844×390): tap a land, the sheet says **Play**, tap it, and the land leaves the hand.
    Long-press a battlefield card and the sheet opens. Tap a spell, **Cast**, the follow-ups are
    answered, and the spell leaves the hand.
  - Classic (390×844): tap to play and long-press to preview.
  - Run with `npm run e2e:table -- touch` / `e2e:classic -- touch`.
- **Exit criterion:** a full game against 3 AIs played on a real phone through the table client, plus a
  few turns on the classic client.
